# Deebo!

So...homelabs are expensive these days. You know what's not expensive? The parts boxes and old laptops that I've been hoarding over the years. 😅 In adversity, we get creative! I've decided to make the homelab equivalent of toilet-wine by "duct taping" all of my old laptops (3), a mini pc, and a full PC that I was able to put together. They will do various things like Minecraft server on one old laptop, Immich on a newer-old laptop, discord bots, home assistant, and maybe even a lightweight LLM.
